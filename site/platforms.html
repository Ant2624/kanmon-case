How you make money.

Customers hit cash gaps in your workflow. They leave for a bank, a broker, or nowhere, or they stall work until cash shows up. Capture that as revenue share on financed volume, without funding the book yourself.

It launches under your name.

Kanmon programs run under your brand. Live examples: Cleo InvoicePay, Cin7 Capital, Avionté SmartFund, PingPong Financing, and Nuvei accounts receivable financing.

Typical path: scope with the team, integrate the web SDK and connect token, place the offer in product, then launch under your brand. Many programs target about one week of implementation and go live within a month once scoping is done. Exact timing depends on your data, UX, and compliance review.
