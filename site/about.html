Small businesses should not have to leave the software they run on to find capital. Kanmon exists so the software can be the answer.

What Kanmon is.

Kanmon is embedded lending infrastructure. You use it to offer financing to the businesses you serve, inside your product and under your brand.

Behind every program, Kanmon is the lender of record. It provides the capital, holds the credit agreement, and assumes the credit risk. It runs identity checks, underwriting, servicing, and collections, so you never have to become a lender yourself.

What Kanmon is not.

Not a marketplace that shops your customers to other lenders. Not a broker that hands them off. Not a credit book your platform has to fund or carry.

One lender, one integration, one company accountable to you and your customers. For a side-by-side view, see Kanmon vs competitors .

Licensed lender of record

A licensed commercial lender, including California DFPI Finance Lenders Law License #60DBO-144925. Kanmon holds the credit agreement and assumes credit risk.

SOC 2 Type I

Independently audited security controls on the lending stack your customers touch through your product.

Soft pull only

Eligibility uses a soft credit pull. Checking never affects a customer's credit score.

KYC, KYB, and AML

Identity and compliance checks run on Kanmon's side, so you do not stand up a lending compliance program.

No credit book for you

Your platform does not fund loans or become the lender of record. Kanmon carries the credit risk on originated programs.

Clear operating split

You own brand, UX, and the customer relationship. Kanmon owns underwriting, funding, servicing, collections, and the credit agreement. See the matrix on For platforms .
