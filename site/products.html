Products

Four products. Four moments.

Every Kanmon product maps to a cash gap your customers already hit inside your platform: term loans, lines of credit, invoice financing, and accounts payable financing.Product set from the kanmon.com FAQ.

Invoice financing

The buyer pays in 60 days. Payroll is Friday.

Who it is for: platforms whose customers carry receivables. Invoicing tools, EDI and supply chain software, staffing, anything with an accounts receivable view.

How it appears: eligible invoices show a financing option directly in the invoice list, at the row that is causing the pain.

Fact: soft credit pull only. Applying does not affect your customers' credit scores.Source: kanmon.com FAQ.

Accounts payable financing

The supplier bill lands before the inventory sells.

Who it is for: supply chain, commerce, and ERP platforms whose customers buy inventory ahead of revenue.

How it appears: a pay-now option on the bill itself, with repayment spread over time.

Fact: your platform does not need embedded payments. Payment data helps underwriting but is not required.Source: kanmon.com FAQ.

Term loan

One big purchase stands between them and the next stage.

Who it is for: practice management and business management platforms whose customers face equipment, buildout, or expansion costs.

How it appears: a fixed offer with an amount and a term, and a short application.

Fact: funds in as little as 24 hours, typically within two days.Source: kanmon.com key features and FAQ.

Line of credit

Cash flow is lumpy. The buffer should not be.

Who it is for: any platform whose customers face recurring timing gaps between work done and cash received.

How it appears: an available-credit widget on the dashboard, drawn as needed, repaid as cash lands.

Fact: tailored options with progressive credit limits.Source: kanmon.com key features.
