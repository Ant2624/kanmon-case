Case studies

Live today, inside partner products.

Five platforms run Kanmon-powered financing under their own brand. Here is what each one shipped.

Supply chain / EDI

Cleo InvoicePay

Cleo embedded invoice financing directly in its WebEDI workflow. A supplier picks the invoices to finance and funds arrive by same-day ACH, with fixed repayments collected automatically.

The moment it serves: goods shipped, invoice issued, buyer paying on net terms.

Cleo's InvoicePay page

Program shape

Invoice financing inside WebEDI · offers within about 3 business days of onboarding · funds in about 24 hours per financed invoice · penalty-free early repayment.

Inventory management

Cin7 Capital

Cin7 launched the full product set: term loans, lines of credit, invoice financing, and accounts payable financing, offered to its inventory-management customers from inside the product.

The moment it serves: inventory to buy before the revenue that pays for it arrives.

Cin7's site

Program shape

All four products under the Cin7 brand · bank connection through Plaid · soft credit pull for eligibility.

Staffing software

Avionté SmartFund

Avionté offers financing to staffing agencies, whose payroll leaves every week while client invoices pay on net terms. Agencies fund the gap without factoring their entire book.

The moment it serves: payroll due Friday, invoices due in 30 to 60 days.

Avionté's site

Program shape

Funding in about 24 to 48 hours · finance selected invoices rather than all-or-nothing · no fees on unused capital.

Cross-border payments

PingPong Financing

PingPong offers term loans and accounts payable financing to its e-commerce sellers, who buy inventory ahead of marketplace payouts.

The moment it serves: restocking for the season before the marketplace pays out.

PingPong's site

Program shape

Up to $250,000 · application in about 5 minutes · funding in about 48 hours · for businesses 12+ months old.

Payments

Nuvei accounts receivable financing

Nuvei offers receivable financing to merchants, enrolled from inside the ERP systems they already use.

The moment it serves: receivables on the books, cash needed now.

Nuvei's site

Program shape

In-ERP enrollment · soft credit pull · offers in about 3 business days.
