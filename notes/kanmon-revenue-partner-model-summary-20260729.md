## When lending happens (one-liners)

1. **Cleo:** WebEDI InvoicePay → finance **unpaid EDI invoices** (30/60/90, ~24h funds) while buyer keeps terms. **[F]**  
2. **PingPong AP:** Pay supplier bills; funds into PingPong account. **[F]**  
3. **PingPong term:** Fixed 6–12 mo loan for growth / inventory peak. **[F]** / peak **[ill]**  
4. **Cin7 AP:** Fund supplier PO from Money → Capital before stock turns. **[F]**  
5. **Cin7 LOC/invoice:** Buffer or advance AR beside inventory ops. **[F]**  
6. **Avionté:** Staffing agency advances selected invoices (payroll vs client pay). **[F]**  
7. **Nuvei:** Merchant advances chosen A/R invoices in payments suite. **[F]**

**Trigger → product:** unpaid AR → invoice · supplier due → AP · lump need → term · ongoing buffer → LOC.

## Cleo path (memorize)

Admin activates InvoicePay → supplier applies to **Kanmon** (UW often ≤24h) → finance option on eligible invoices → pick invoices + 30/60/90 → funds ~24h · login WebEDI **or** `cleo.kanmon.com` · soft pull / bank / $25/$35 on related FAQs.

## Why partners embed

Rev share + **no credit risk** + sticky capital-in-workflow + (Kanmon claim) lower churn / higher ARPU.

---

## PM implications

- Kanmon $ ↑ with **funded volume × yield − losses − partner share**  
- Partner happiness ↑ with **in-workflow cash at real moments** + fair share + brand-safe intensity  
- Don’t trade credit quality for vanity originations
