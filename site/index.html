Get the answers you need.

What products can we offer our customers?

Term loans, lines of credit, invoice financing, and accounts payable financing. Programs typically start with the product that matches your customers' most common cash gap and expand from there.

Who provides the capital?

Kanmon does. Kanmon is the lender of record: it provides the capital, holds the credit agreement, and assumes all credit risk. Your platform never lends its own money.

Is Kanmon a licensed lender?

Yes. Kanmon is a licensed commercial lender, including California DFPI Finance Lenders Law License #60DBO-144925.

Do we need embedded payments first?

No. Payment data helps underwriting, but it is not required. Platforms without embedded payments run Kanmon programs today.

How fast do customers get funded?

Most businesses apply in under 10 minutes. Funds arrive in as little as 24 hours, typically within two days.

Will applying affect our customers' credit scores?

No. Kanmon uses a soft credit pull for eligibility, which does not affect credit scores.

How do we get started?

Talk to Kanmon, scope the program, and implement in about one week. Most programs are live within a month.
