## Money + motive (10 sec)

**SMB** pays fees for speed / soft pull / stay-in-workflow / invoice·AP control.  
**Partner** gets rev-share / bps pitch (**LinkedIn**; **% unpublished**) + retention / no credit risk. Not a FAQ line.  
**Kanmon** gets yield − losses − share − ops. Biggest fight: **volume vs risk vs brand spam**.

## Case implications (Capital Moments)

1. Moments = align SMB pain + partner brand, not louder banners.  
2. Kill Moments if stall ≠ placement (ops/incentive/credit).  
3. Capability gaps fit Series A PM better than blind incentive spend, *unless* funnel already converts.  
4. Bet Moments on **invoice/AP objects + routing**, not generic “right time.”
