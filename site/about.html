About

A licensed lender,
built as infrastructure.

Small businesses should not have to leave the software they run on to find capital. Kanmon exists so the software can be the answer.

What Kanmon is.

Kanmon is embedded lending infrastructure. Software platforms use it to offer financing to the businesses they serve, inside their own product and under their own brand.

Behind every program, Kanmon is the lender of record. It provides the capital, holds the credit agreement, and assumes the credit risk. It runs identity checks, underwriting, servicing, and collections, so its partners never have to become lenders themselves.

What Kanmon is not.

Not a marketplace that shops your customers to other lenders. Not a broker that hands them off. Not a balance-sheet risk your platform carries.

One lender, one integration, one company accountable to you and your customers.
